▶ Video Player

Video Player is a personal Windows desktop app made by Jack Zicrosky for his own use. It plays video recordings and helps publish them and run live streams on his own YouTube and Twitch channels.

  • Plays videos, including recordings with several audio tracks.
  • Uploads videos to the owner's YouTube channels.
  • Manages the owner's YouTube and Twitch live streams (titles, thumbnails, starting and ending streams, chat).

The app runs entirely on the owner's PC. It isn't sold or offered as a service.
